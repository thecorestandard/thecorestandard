The Problem

A candidate can interview with poise and fall apart in the first week.

CVs tell you where someone has been. Interviews tell you how they present. Neither tells you how they’ll actually handle your priorities, your stakeholders, or the pressure of the role.

The Core Standard puts candidates inside a realistic working day and scores them across five dimensions that actually predict success.

The Core Standard The framework What we
measure

Not a quiz. Not a personality test. We score how a candidate actually behaves inside a realistic working day. The five below are the core; senior roles add dimensions suited to the seat, such as Partnership, Discretion, and Commercial Awareness.

Behaviour,
not credentials.
  1. 01
    Under pressure

    Prioritisation

    When the inbox, the diary and three "urgent" requests land at once, do they protect what the principal actually needs first, and know what can wait?

  2. 02
    Signal & nuance

    Judgement

    Do they catch the clash in the diary, the risk buried in an email, and the thing the principal hasn't said yet, and act before it becomes a problem?

  3. 03
    Tone & audience

    Communication

    Can they write in the principal's voice, pitch the tone right for a board member or a difficult supplier, and say no without causing offence?

  4. 04
    Plan to delivery

    Execution

    Do they turn a half-sentence brief into a finished result, chase the loose ends, and close the loop so nothing slips through?

  5. 05
    Trust & proxy

    Partnership

    Do they work as a genuine partner, not just a pair of hands? Anticipating what the principal needs before it is asked, holding relationships and stakeholders on their behalf, and earning the trust to act in their name.

Inside the simulation

What the candidate actually does.

No multiple choice, no typing tests. They are dropped into a realistic EA working day and have to make real decisions, in the order a real day throws them. You see how they think, not what they have rehearsed.

3

Communicate

The Employer Report

Comprehensive Insight

Your candidate sits the simulation; you get a written report that breaks down how someone performed across the five core pillars: not just what someone chose, but how they approached it. Expert-reviewed and in your inbox within 48 hours, built to sit alongside your interview notes and references.

Prioritisation. What gets done first when everything is urgent, and whether the principal’s agenda stays front of mind.
Judgement. The risk buried in a meeting request, the conflict in the diary, the tone shift in an email.
Communication. Writing in the principal’s voice: concise, warm, and right for the audience.
Execution. Closing the loop: confirming receipt, flagging blockers, leaving the day tidier than it was found.
Partnership. Operating as a trusted extension of the principal: anticipating needs, holding stakeholders and relationships on their behalf, and earning the latitude to act for them.

Those five are scored at every level. Higher levels add further competencies matched to the seat: AI Fluency, Mindset, Judgement & Ethics, and Data Interpretation.

  • Each pillar scored 0–100, with an overall band
  • Narrative feedback on decision-making patterns
  • Assessor recommendation calibrated to the level you are hiring
  • Detailed notes on risk factors and strengths
  • Benchmark comparison (top / median / developing)

A wrong EA hire costs far more than the salary. There is the recruitment fee, the weeks of onboarding, the work that slips while the seat is not functioning, and then the cost of running the whole search again. This report is how you avoid it: hard evidence on the shortlist you have already narrowed by interview, for a fraction of the cost of getting it wrong.

An immersive simulation of a realistic EA working day. Your candidate is dropped into a timed scenario, 60 to 90 minutes depending on the level, covering inbox triage, priority management, conflict navigation, and stakeholder communication. The result is a competency report covering Prioritisation, Judgement, Communication, Execution, and Partnership. It is not a quiz, a personality test, or a psychometric.
Interviews show how someone presents. Psychometrics describe self-reported tendencies. Neither captures how a person actually thinks under pressure or what they would do with your inbox at 9am. The Core Standard captures behaviour, in real scenarios, at the level you are hiring for. Most clients use it alongside interviews. Not instead of them.
Between 60 and 90 minutes, depending on level: Level 1 is 60, Level 2 is 75, and Level 3 is 90. Every session is a hard stop, so the candidate cannot pause or over-run. Reports are delivered within 48 hours of completion. Setup on your side takes under 5 minutes: send a name, an email, and the level you want assessed.
Every assessment is expert reviewed. Lily Shippen reviews and signs off on every employer report. The scoring framework is calibrated by people who hire and manage EAs every week, not an algorithm in isolation.
Yes. We are committed to a fair assessment for every candidate. No diagnosis or formal evidence is required; self-certification is enough. The scenario, scoring, and reviewer process stay identical; only the time allowance changes. Candidates or employers can email hello@thecorestandard.co.uk to arrange it, and we are happy to discuss any adjustment not covered here.
