Why We Built This

30 years. Both sides of the desk.

In 13 years of specialist EA recruitment, the question I was asked more than any other by clients was: "How do you test your candidates?" At Lily Shippen we heavily vet every candidate we place: but there was always a missing piece. Interviews test confidence and presentation. They don't reveal how someone triages a chaotic inbox at 8am, handles a last-minute board paper, or navigates a sensitive situation with a difficult stakeholder.

The Core Standard fills that gap. Every assessment is a simulation of a realistic working day, designed from real situations we've seen play out across hundreds of placements. It's built on what actually predicts success in the role: not what looks good across a desk.

Lily Shippen recruits. eavolve develops. The Core Standard assesses. Together, an ecosystem that supports EAs and the businesses that rely on them: from hire, through development, to measurable performance.
