What the test measures

Five pillars. One day of work.

A realistic working day, scored against the dimensions that actually predict success in the seat, not how a candidate performs in an interview room.

The Core Standard The framework What we
measure

Not a quiz. Not a personality test. We score how a candidate behaves across a realistic working day. The five below are the core; higher levels add dimensions suited to the seat.

Behaviour,
not credentials.
  1. 01
    Under pressure

    Prioritisation

    Does the candidate recognise what matters most when everything is urgent, and hold the principal's agenda in mind when sequencing the day?

  2. 02
    Signal & nuance

    Judgement

    Do they spot the risk buried in the meeting request, the conflict in the diary, the tone shift in the email? The unflashy work of noticing.

  3. 03
    Tone & audience

    Communication

    Can they write with the voice of the principal? Concise, warm, appropriate to the audience. Poor writing costs relationships.

  4. 04
    Plan to delivery

    Execution

    Do they close the loop? Confirm receipt, flag blockers, and leave the day tidier than they found it. The end-of-day discipline.

  5. 05
    Trust & proxy

    Partnership

    Do they work as a genuine partner, not just a pair of hands? Anticipating what the principal needs before it is asked, holding relationships and stakeholders on their behalf, and earning the trust to act in their name.

The five pillars are scored at every level. Because the test spans three levels of seniority, the higher levels layer on further dimensions matched to the demands of the seat.

Why it pays for itself

A wrong EA hire costs far more than the salary.

Add the recruitment fee to find them, the onboarding and training, the principal’s time spent re-recruiting, the disruption to the day, and the momentum lost while the seat sits wrong, then the cost of running the whole search again. An assessment is £495 to £995: a small fraction of that, and it turns a high-stakes judgement call into an evidenced one, on the shortlist you have already narrowed by interview.
