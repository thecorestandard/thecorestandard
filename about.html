About

Thirty years. Both sides of the desk.

The Core Standard was built by people who have placed, trained, and worked as Executive Assistants. We know what the role demands. We know what interviews miss. We built the assessment we wish we'd had for the last thirteen years.

Origin

Thirteen years of placements. One recurring problem.

Across thirteen years of placing Executive Assistants and Private PAs, Lily Shippen has seen the same pattern repeat. Candidates who interviewed with polish but collapsed in the first week, alongside candidates who did not present well in the room but went on to hold a complex seat with quiet authority.

The real issue facing the profession has never been whether EAs are capable. The issue is that the industry has lacked a common language for describing what good actually looks like. Interviews test confidence and rehearsal; they cannot catch judgement under pressure, the micro-decisions that define a good day, or the discretion that makes a principal's life work. The result is expensive mis-hires and unfair outcomes for candidates who happen to interview less well than they operate.

The Core Standard was built to close that gap. It does not replace the interview. It adds the piece the interview cannot reach: a realistic working day, measured.

The Team

Built by specialists.

The Core Standard is a Lily Shippen business, co-founded with Emily Partridge. Between them, nearly thirty years of hiring, placing, and working in the seat at the highest level of the EA profession.

What We Believe

Four principles.

These are not marketing lines. They are the decisions that shaped how the assessment was built, and the standard we hold ourselves to with every report we sign off.

Approach over answers.

We do not test what a candidate knows. We test how they think. Judgement, not recall.

Evidence over intuition.

Every score is backed by the candidate's own words. No black box, and no unfalsifiable claims.

Expert reviewed.

Nothing goes out machine only. A specialist recruiter signs off every employer report. Always.

Fair to both sides.

Candidates deserve a fair, rigorous test of what they actually do. Employers deserve signal they can trust. We build for both.
