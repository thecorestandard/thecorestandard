Why Certified exists

EA & PA certification is overdue.

There is no professional body that tests whether you can do the job. There is no examination that asks you to handle a real inbox, a real diary, a real curveball. Until now, your practice was evidenced only by the opinion of your last principal and the fluency of your interview answers.

TCS Certified changes that. The same rubric, the same pillars, the same reviewer standard: on a bespoke scenario built for the candidate edition, available to you without needing an employer to commission it. You sit the assessment, receive the report, and decide who sees it.

What you receive

Four pieces of evidence. One inbox.

Most EAs have nothing beyond their last reference to evidence how they actually work. These four deliverables travel with you into every application, every conversation with a recruiter, every LinkedIn scroll-past, and every reason someone might need to verify what you say.

Deliverable 03

The Certificate

A signed digital certificate: your name, your level, your overall band, the date, and the issuing reviewer. Every certificate carries a unique verifier URL. Any employer or recruiter can confirm it with one click, at any time, without needing to contact us.

Helps you
Prove your standard without needing a reference call. Give employers something they can trust on sight. Carry a credential that is not tied to any one company you have worked for.
Deliverable 04

The Digital Badge

A shareable, verifiable badge designed to live anywhere your name appears online. Links back to your verifier URL automatically. Distinct visual markers for each level, so recruiters recognise the tier at a glance.

Helps you
Show up in recruiter searches. Signal calibre before anyone reads a word. Turn your LinkedIn profile and email signature into passive marketing every time someone opens them.

What your result means

Three levels of the standard.

You sit one assessment, and your work places you at one of three levels. It is the level you demonstrated on the day, scored by the same reviewers and the same rubric an employer commissions against.

Level 1

Professional

Keeps an executive's working life running accurately and calmly. Diary, inbox, travel and coordination handled reliably, so nothing slips.

Level 2

Trusted Partner

A true right hand. Anticipates, prioritises, manages stakeholders, and holds things together under pressure.

Level 3

Executive Partner

A strategic partner who reads the commercial picture and operates as an extension of leadership.

Your report shows the level you reached and, just as usefully, exactly what would take you to the next one.
