How First Look works

Three modules. One reviewer. Two pages.

01

You commission

Enter the candidate’s name and email and pay £195 +VAT by card. The invitation goes out from hello@thecorestandard.co.uk within one working day, with a single-use link valid for 14 days.

02

The candidate sits it

Thirty minutes with a hard stop, in their own time. The morning inbox, a diary that falls apart, and a reply in the principal’s voice, all set in the same “Ashworth Partners” scenario as Level 1 of the full assessment.

03

A reviewer scores it

Each module is scored against the published rubric on the five pillars: Prioritisation, Judgement, Communication, Execution and Partnership. The reviewer reads every answer and signs the report before it goes out.

04

Two pages, within 48 hours

Pillar scores, the reviewer’s note, and a steer on what to do next. If you commission the full assessment on the same candidate within 30 days, the £195 is deducted and the candidate sits only the modules they have not yet seen.

What lands in your inbox

A two-page report, not a twelve-page one.

Page one: the scores. Five pillar scores on the same 0 to 100 benchmark used in every Core Standard report, so a First Look result reads directly against a full assessment or a Recruiter Summary. Each score carries a one-line reason drawn from what the candidate actually did.

Page two: the reviewer’s note. What stood out, what to probe at interview, and a plain steer: proceed to interview, run the full assessment, or look elsewhere. The reviewer signs it. There is no hire recommendation at a level, because three modules are not enough to give one honestly. That is what the full assessment is for.
