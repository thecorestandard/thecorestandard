EA Hire

Individual executive assistant hire

One assessment for every candidate. A realistic 97-minute working day, scored by a reviewer, with the level they demonstrated and a hire recommendation back within 48 hours. Built for employers running a targeted EA search. One payment, one candidate, one report.

  • £495 +VAT per candidate
  • Places the candidate at Level 1, 2 or 3 from their work
  • Priority 24-hour turnaround available at checkout

For existing teams Live

Benchmark Your Team

Assess the EAs you already have

Not a hire. Each EA sits the Certified assessment at their level. You get a team report tailored to you, each person's own report and development plan, and for teams of five or more a 30-minute debrief with Lily. Priced per person, cheaper the more of the team you assess.

  • 1 to 4 people · £225 pp +VAT
  • 5 to 9 people · £200 pp +VAT
  • 10 to 19 people · £185 pp +VAT
  • 20+ people · £175 pp +VAT

Sit the candidate edition of the assessment. Same rubric, same reviewer team, same standard as the employer version. All on your own terms. You keep the report, the recruiter summary, the certificate, and the verifiable digital badge.
