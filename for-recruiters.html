For recruitment agencies placing EAs, PAs and Chiefs of Staff

The assessment your shortlist
arrives with.

I have run a specialist EA recruitment agency for thirteen years, and I still do. The hardest conversation was never finding the candidate. It was the client saying “they all look the same on paper”, and the interview that followed proving her right. The Core Standard is what I wanted to hand over with every shortlist: a scenario-based assessment, scored against a published rubric, reviewed by a specialist, with the report in the client’s hands inside 48 hours. What it does for an agency

Four things I want on my own desk.

Win the pitch

Evidence, not promises

A retained mandate is won on process. When the process includes a reviewer-signed competency report on every shortlisted candidate, you are no longer pitching your judgement against a competitor’s. You are pitching evidence.

Protect the placement

Fewer rebates

Rebates are paid on the hires that go wrong in month two, and those are the hires that interviewed well. A report on how a candidate actually handled a working day catches the gap between presenting and performing before the offer, not after.

Sell the shortlist

Three reports, not three CVs

The client sees the pillar scores side by side, reads the reviewer’s note on each candidate, and goes into the final interview knowing where to probe. Your shortlist stops looking like everyone else’s.

Keep the margin

Bill it your way

Pass it through to the client at cost, add it to a retained fee, or absorb it as the thing that sets you apart. Partner rates start 20% below list from the first assessment. However you bill it, you own the client.

How it works for you

Four steps.

1. You commission. Choose the level, enter the candidate’s name and email, and pay by card with your partner code. You receive the invoice. If you would rather pass the cost through, send the client the public link and they pay list price directly.

2. The candidate sits it. They receive a single-use link from hello@thecorestandard.co.uk, valid for 14 days, and sit a realistic working day of 60 to 90 minutes depending on level. It is text-based and asynchronous, with no live interviewer, and it is built to reflect the real role and the value of it, so a strong candidate is engaged by it rather than put off. That matters to you more than to anyone. A test that insults a good EA costs you the EA.

3. We score and review. Every submission is scored against the five pillars and reviewed by me before release. Reviewer-signed within 48 hours, or 24 with priority turnaround.

4. You receive the report. It comes to you as the commissioner, with a co-branded cover at retainer tier. Forward it with the shortlist, or walk the client through it.

Rules of engagement

Built on trust, with no conflict of interest.

I founded Lily Shippen Recruitment and still run it, so I know the first question another agency asks: why would I put my candidates through an assessment run by a recruiter? The answer is that The Core Standard is not the agency. It is part of eavolve, a separate business with its own team and its own data, and it exists to become the recognised benchmark for EAs, not to feed a recruitment desk. Lily Shippen Recruitment uses it on the same partner terms as any other agency.

The rules are simple, and I will put them in writing to any client of yours who asks.

  • You own the client relationship. We never approach a client you have introduced, for follow-on work or anything else.
  • We never approach a candidate you have assessed. Nothing that passes through The Core Standard is shared with any recruitment business, mine included.
  • The report goes to whoever commissioned it. If that is you, the client sees it when you share it.
  • Candidates are told what the assessment is for and how their responses will be used. Their data is held in UK and EU data centres, never used to train AI, and deleted after twelve months.
  • Your partner rate is for assessments you commission for your own clients. It is not for resale to other agencies.
