Why Private PA is its own stream

A different principal. A different day.

An Executive Assistant supports a principal's business operations. A Private PA supports their life. The remit is broader and the stakes are more personal. A Private PA coordinates contractors and estate managers, liaises with house staff, manages family travel that includes minors, handles medical appointments, steers gift-giving protocol, and buffers the principal from a hundred small demands a day that would never reach a corporate inbox.

The judgment calls are different too. When a family matter collides with a business meeting, the Private PA decides who moves. When a staff dispute risks escalating, they draft the message. When a supplier tries to name-drop the principal to win other work, they shut it down.

Our EA assessments do not test for this. The Private PA stream does.
