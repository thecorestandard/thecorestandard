Insights · EA hiring, testing & assessment

What we notice when we assess an EA.

Thirteen years recruiting Executive Assistants, Private PAs and Chiefs of Staff, and now hundreds of them scored through the assessment. This is what that work teaches us: how the strongest candidates think under pressure, where capable ones come unstuck, and how to see the difference before you make the hire. Written for the people hiring, and for the people sitting the assessment who want to know what we are actually looking for.

Most of the writing on Executive Assistant hiring online is either thin SEO content or vendor marketing dressed up as insight. The pieces below are different: they are drawn from thirteen years of specialist EA recruitment and from sitting on both sides of a Chief of Staff and EA Manager desk. Every article is written to help someone make a better decision, whether that is commissioning a Core Standard assessment for a shortlist, sitting TCS Certified to demonstrate your own capability, or understanding how a structured, scenario-based assessment works in practice.

We publish a new piece roughly every month. If there is a question you think should be answered here and is not, email us and it will likely end up in the queue.
