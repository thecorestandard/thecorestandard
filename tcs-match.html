Who TCS Match is for

The hires where fit is the difference between one year and seven.

Some hires you can make on capability alone. TCS Match is not built for those. It is built for the hires where the working relationship is unusually close, the visibility into the principal's life is unusually high, and the intended tenure is years rather than months. Get the fit wrong on these and a candidate who passed every interview, scored cleanly on capability, and looked right on paper still leaves before they have paid back the cost of hiring them.

We do not recommend TCS Match as a standalone. Without the capability data underneath, it is a social compatibility check, not a hiring signal. Used with the capability assessment, it becomes the second layer that turns a strong hire into a durable one.

How TCS Match works

Two questionnaires. One overlay.

01

Candidate questionnaire

Twenty minutes, sixty questions, alongside the Core Standard. Covers preferred pace, feedback rhythm, decision approach, communication register, autonomy needs, and structure preference. Calibrated to the principal-EA dynamic specifically, not a generic personality framework.

02

Principal questionnaire

Ten minutes, completed by the hiring principal or their proxy (often an existing EA or COO). Asks about the operating environment rather than self-perception: how fast is the day, how directive is the comms, how much visibility do they want? That data moves the outcome. Self-rating doesn't.

03

Compatibility mapping

A reviewer pairs the two questionnaires across the six dimensions. Some reward closeness (pace, register), others reward complementarity (decision style, structure). The reviewer names where the fit is tight, where it's complementary, and where it deserves a final-stage conversation before offer.

04

Added to your report

A four-page supplement to the Core Standard employer report. Compatibility score, dimension-by-dimension read, the conversations worth having before offer, and an onboarding note for the first ninety days. Delivered in the same 48-hour window as the capability report.

What TCS Match maps

Six dimensions of working compatibility.

Pace

How fast the principal operates versus how fast the candidate is comfortable operating. Closeness rewarded.

Mismatch surfaces at week three. A fast principal experiences a careful candidate as slow. A careful principal experiences a fast candidate as reckless.

Communication register

Formal, direct, warm, or terse. The candidate has to write in the principal's voice. Closeness rewarded.

Mismatch surfaces in the first sent email. The fastest signal a hire isn't working.

Decision style

Evidence-led versus instinct-led. Often complementarity rewarded: an instinct-led principal benefits from an evidence-led second brain, and vice versa.

Mismatch shows up as friction in approval cycles. The principal asks for a recommendation and gets a deck, or asks for analysis and gets a hunch.

Feedback preferences

How direct, how frequent, how public. Mismatch here wears on the relationship fastest.

Mismatch shows up as feedback that lands as criticism, or doesn't land at all. The most common cause of a candidate quietly opening LinkedIn at month nine.

Structure preference

Tightly structured days or emergent. Often complementarity rewarded: a highly emergent principal benefits from a structured counterpart.

Mismatch shows up as a structured principal who feels chaotic, or an emergent principal who feels micromanaged.

Autonomy tolerance

How much visibility the principal wants versus how much autonomy the candidate needs to do good work. The single biggest driver of tenure.

Mismatch shows up as a principal who feels out of the loop, or a candidate who feels watched. Either way, the relationship doesn't last.

When to add TCS Match

A quick yes or no.

Not every hire needs TCS Match. For a short-tenure contract EA, a household PA on a rota, or a Chief of Staff on a defined 12-month project, the capability assessment is usually enough. Where TCS Match earns its place is the close-quarters, long-tenure hire where capability and fit are both load-bearing.

Add Match

  • ·  Dedicated EA to one principal, tenure 2+ years
  • ·  EA to Senior Leadership or Executive Business Partner (L2 or L3)
  • ·  Family Office or UHNW Private PA
  • ·  Chief of Staff on an open-ended mandate
  • ·  Replacement for a long-tenure incumbent

Skip Match

  • ·  Short-term contract or maternity cover
  • ·  Rota-based household PA team
  • ·  Project-scope Chief of Staff (defined end date)
  • ·  Junior EA in a multi-EA team with rotation
