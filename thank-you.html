What happens next

1

A receipt from Stripe

Check your inbox for a payment confirmation from Stripe. Keep it for your records, it doubles as a VAT invoice.

2

A short briefing form

Within one working day we will email you a short briefing form. For employers: the candidate’s name, email, and the role context. For candidates sitting Certified: just a name-and-date confirmation.

3

The assessment link

Once the briefing is confirmed, a single-use assessment link goes out to the candidate. The link is valid for 14 days. Desktop browser, quiet environment, one sitting.

4

The report, within 48 hours

Once the candidate submits, the report is AI-scored, reviewer-signed-off, and delivered to your inbox within 48 hours. 24 hours if you added Priority Turnaround at checkout.
